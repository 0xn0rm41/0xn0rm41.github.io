OSINT // Geolocation

Exercise 001 — Kiffa, Mauritania

Geolocation analysis of an open-source image using anchor point methodology, historical satellite imagery, and solar shadow verification.

ANALYST n0rm41
DATE 03/04/2026
SOURCE gralhix.com/osint-exercise-001
STATUS RESOLVED
01

Initial Observation

The image depicts a sparsely developed urban area in a semi-arid environment. No road signs, readable text, or distinct landmarks are immediately visible. The following elements were catalogued from the raw image:

  • A building bearing Arabic script, consistent with a mosque or Islamic institution
  • Several individuals wearing traditional Islamic clothing
  • Three low-density housing structures of simple construction
  • A paved road with a visible curve — anomalous for the environment
  • A localised green area, atypical for a desert-adjacent setting
  • No readable signage, vehicle plates, or distinctive infrastructure
02

Hypothesis Formation

The initial geographic hypothesis — Kiffa, Mauritania — was derived from convergence of four independent signals:


  • Arabic script is consistent with Mauritania's official language and script
  • Traditional clothing aligns with West African Islamic cultural norms
  • Semi-arid environment with minimal vegetation matches Kiffa's climate profile
  • Low-density, simple urban layout is characteristic of secondary Mauritanian cities
03

Anchor Points Identified

Cultural
Arabic script → Islamic / NW African context
Cultural

Geographic

Temporal

04

Search Methodology

Google Earth Pro SunCalc Historical Imagery

01 Opened Google Earth Pro and loaded historical imagery for 2013 over Kiffa, Mauritania
02 Filtered visible area by two constraints: presence of vegetation and presence of paved roads — both rare in the region
03 Narrowed the search zone to a small subset of the city matching both environmental constraints
04 Cross-referenced road curvature and building layout against satellite imagery
05 Ran shadow verification using SunCalc at the candidate coordinates and timestamp
