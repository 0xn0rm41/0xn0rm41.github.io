Exercise 003 — Ankara, Turkey

Identification of a diplomatic meeting location via architectural analysis, Google dorking, and open-source event documentation corroboration.

ANALYST n0rm41
DATE 04/04/2026
SOURCE gralhix.com/osint-exercise-003
STATUS RESOLVED
// Task Brief
In April 2017, Mohamed Abdullahi Farmaajo, the then president of Somalia, visited Turkey. A news agency published a photograph showing him shaking hands with Recep Tayyip Erdoğan. The article did not disclose where the photo was taken. Objective: identify the name and coordinates of the location.
01

Initial Observation

The image depicts two individuals shaking hands in front of an ornate door, in what is clearly a formal diplomatic setting. No readable signage is visible. The following elements were catalogued:

Date visible in image April 27, 2017 — visible at top of image frame
// Identified Subjects
President of Somalia Mohamed Abdullahi Farmaajo
President of Turkey (host) Recep Tayyip Erdoğan
  • Ornate door with golden circular decorative pattern and red central element
  • Somali and Turkish flags visible in frame — bilateral diplomatic context confirmed
  • Formal attire consistent with a head-of-state meeting
  • No street-level context, road signs, or external architectural elements visible
02

Hypothesis Formation

The initial hypothesis — T.C. Cumhurbaşkanlığı Külliyesi, Ankara (Turkish Presidential Complex) — was formed on the following reasoning chain:


  • Both subjects are heads of state → meeting almost certainly at an official presidential venue
  • Host country is Turkey → location is within Turkish territory
  • The most prominent presidential venue in Turkey is the Presidential Complex in Ankara
  • The ornate door's decorative pattern — golden circular motifs and a red central element — is architecturally consistent with the Complex's interior ceremonial spaces
  • Presence of both national flags on equal standing → formal bilateral reception protocol
03

Anchor Points Identified

Primary — Flags
Somali + Turkish flags → bilateral diplomatic event in Turkey
Primary — Temporal
Date visible in frame: April 27, 2017 → enables event-based search
Architectural
Golden circular door pattern + red central element → presidential complex interior
Protocol
Formal attire + symmetric flag placement → head-of-state reception
04

Search Methodology

Google Dorking Google Maps Google Earth Pro 3D Imagery

// Dork Query — Event Reconstruction
"Farmaajo" "Erdogan" "April 2017" "Turkey" site:twitter.com OR site:allafrica.com
Result: Single confirmed location — T.C. Cumhurbaşkanlığı Külliyesi, Ankara

01 Applied Google dork combining subject names, date, and country to surface event documentation from news archives and social media
02 Dork returned a single candidate location — the Presidential Complex (T.C. Cumhurbaşkanlığı Külliyesi), Yenimahalle, Ankara
03 Queried the complex on Google Maps to confirm geographic position and surrounding layout
04 Loaded Google Earth Pro with 3D building imagery — cross-referenced visible door patterns, symmetry, and architectural details against image frame
05 Located the specific ceremonial entrance consistent with the door's decorative pattern — confirmed geometric and stylistic match
05

Confirmation

Building architecture matches 3D imagery
Door pattern and symmetry confirmed
Event documented by multiple open sources
Flag protocol consistent with venue
