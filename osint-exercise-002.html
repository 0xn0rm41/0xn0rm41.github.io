OSINT // Geolocation

Exercise 002 — Melbourne, Australia

Station and building identification using visible signage, historical satellite imagery, and multi-source height verification with documented source conflict.

ANALYST n0rm41
DATE 04/04/2026
SOURCE gralhix.com/osint-exercise-002
STATUS RESOLVED
01

Initial Observation

The image depicts a dense urban environment consistent with a major Anglophone city. The following elements were catalogued from the raw image:

  • A railway station with its name partially visible on signage
  • Multiple mid-to-high-rise commercial and residential buildings
  • A localised green area within an otherwise built-up environment
  • Individuals in casual Western clothing
  • No visible road signs, vehicle plates, or secondary text anchors

Tasked objectives:

  • Identify the name of the station
  • Identify the tallest building visible and determine its height
02

Hypothesis Formation

The initial geographic hypothesis — Melbourne, Victoria, Australia — was derived from convergence of three independent signals:


  • Station signage contained the text "Flinders" — consistent with Flinders Street Station, Melbourne
  • English-language signage and Anglo-Saxon urban architecture
  • Building density and skyline profile consistent with a major Australian CBD
03

Search Methodology

Google Maps Google Earth Pro Historical Imagery Skyscraper Center

⚠ Methodological Limitation
No date or time metadata was available for this image. Solar shadow analysis (SunCalc) was therefore not applicable and could not be used as a confirmation vector.

01 Queried "Flinders Street Station" — result confirmed Flinders Street Station, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
02 Cross-referenced station location on Google Maps to confirm surrounding skyline matches image composition
03 Identified a building visible in the image that no longer appears in current satellite imagery
04 Loaded Google Earth Pro historical imagery — confirmed the removed building was present in earlier captures, validating the location independently of signage
05 Identified the tallest structure in the frame as Focus Melbourne (Southbank) and queried Skyscraper Center for height data
